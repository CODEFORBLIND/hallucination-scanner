The 2023 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup was the 13th edition of the tournament, hosted entirely by India from October 5 to November 19, 2023. In the final match held at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad in front of 92,453 spectators, Australia defeated India by 6 wickets to claim their record-extending sixth World Cup title. India batted first and was bowled out for 240 runs in 50 overs, with KL Rahul top-scoring with 66 runs. In response, Australia chased down the target in 43 overs, finishing at 241 for 4, driven by a magnificent 137-run knock from Travis Head, who was named Player of the Match.

During the 2023 tournament, India's Virat Kohli set a new record for the most runs in a single World Cup edition, amassing 765 runs across 11 innings at an average of 95.62, surpassing Sachin Tendulkar's 2003 record of 673 runs. Indian fast bowler Mohammed Shami was the leading wicket-taker of the tournament with 24 wickets in just 7 matches, including a best bowling figure of 7 for 57 against New Zealand in the semi-final at Wankhede Stadium. South Africa's Quinton de Kock hit the most centuries, scoring 4 in the group stage, before retiring from ODI cricket.

The following year, in the 2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup co-hosted by the West Indies and the United States, India secured their second T20 title by defeating South Africa by 7 runs in a thrilling final at Kensington Oval, Barbados, on June 29, 2024. Defending a total of 176 for 7, Indian bowlers Jasprit Bumrah and Hardik Pandya restricted South Africa to 169 for 8. Rohit Sharma captained the victorious Indian side and subsequently announced his retirement from T20 Internationals alongside Virat Kohli and Ravindra Jadeja.

In the domestic franchise circuit, the 2024 Women's Premier League (WPL) concluded with Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB) winning their maiden title. Captained by Smriti Mandhana, RCB defeated Delhi Capitals by 8 wickets in the final at the Arun Jaitley Stadium in New Delhi on March 17, 2024. Australian all-rounder Ellyse Perry won the Orange Cap for scoring the most runs (347) in the season, while Shreyanka Patil took the Purple Cap with 13 wickets.
